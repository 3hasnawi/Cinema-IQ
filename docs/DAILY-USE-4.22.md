# Daily-use features (batch 2)

- **Play on a series resumes correctly**: the episode in progress, else the one after the last finished, else the first. The button reads "استئناف الحلقة N" / "تشغيل الحلقة N".
- **Continue-watching options**: long-press a card (TV: hold OK) to remove it (also unfollows the series) or mark it watched. Long-press on TV is not yet verified on a device.
- **Playback speed**: "الجودة والسرعة" panel has a speed row cycling 0.75× – 2×; it is kept across episodes.
- **Auto next episode**: after an episode ends an 8 s countdown starts the next one. Turn it off in Settings → Playback (TV) or Settings (phone).
- **TV**: the player error box now offers retry for source errors; the two dead trailer-preview settings were replaced by the auto-next row.

No APK is built by this change.
